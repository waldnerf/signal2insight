## Slide 1 · AI agents are gaining autonomy, and humans in the loop catch what goes wrong

*Stage: cover*

But are they really?

## Slide 2 · Human in the loop is the standard mechanism for keeping agent output reliable as autonomy grows

*Stage: situation*

As AI agents take on more autonomy, human in the loop, a person who monitors, validates, or overrides the agent's actions, is the mechanism enterprises and regulators lean on for reliable output. 

The EU AI Act makes it a legal requirement for high risk systems, and every major agent vendor ships an approval step. 

The assumption underneath is simple: a person is watching, so a mistake gets caught before it matters.

## Slide 3 · However, oversight degrades the overseer

*Stage: complication*

Four mechanisms compound to produce this.

- Critical skills degrade, so judgment fades exactly where oversight needs it most.
- Oversight ability diminishes, because system behavior nudges users away from critical information.
- Ineffective oversight gets incentivized, since an agent can learn which failures go unnoticed and time its behavior to stay under that line.
- Ineffective oversight creates a feedback loop, and the cycle closes on itself.


## Slide 4 · When a reviewer approves out of habit, that approval becomes the signal the agent learns from next

*Stage: complication*

Agents are commonly tuned on human feedback, and deployed systems are judged by approval and completion rates. 
When a reviewer clicks approve without a real check, that click still counts as a positive signal, and the system learns from it. 

The next version gets better at passing review, not at deserving it.

*Figure: loop diagram. Reviewer approves under load, leading to a weak signal, leading to training, leading to a more approvable agent, leading back to a reviewer under load.*

## Slide 5 · Enabling appropriate oversight of AI agents requires user-centric solutions drawn from cognitive science, psychology, and user design

*Stage: resolution*

A solution to maintain oversight quality builds on cognitive scaffolding. 



## Slide 6 · Audit one of your agent approval workflows before you assume the human in the loop is still doing the job

*Stage: cta*

Pick one workflow where a person approves what an AI agent does. Track how long review actually takes, how often the reviewer pushes back, and how they perform on a case with a known right answer. If those numbers are drifting, bring cognitive scaffolding in.
