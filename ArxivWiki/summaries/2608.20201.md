# The Third Restructuring of Software Form: From the Three-Tier Architecture to Storage, Models, and Agents

## High-Level Summary

This position paper argues software architecture is undergoing a third paradigm shift -- from the classic three-tier (UI / business logic / data) architecture to a new form built on three elements: a generalized database (all persistent state and memory), a large model (the reasoning core), and an agent (the execution loop connecting them).

## Key Highlights

- Argues the UI layer gets absorbed by the model's ability to generate interfaces on demand, and the business-logic layer gets re-partitioned by how 'expressible' the logic is.
- Frames the shift as the third in software's history, after Software 1.0 (instructions determine behavior) and Software 2.0 (data/ML determines behavior).
- A conceptual argument, not an empirical result -- offered as a structural lens for platform strategy, not a validated technique.

## Why It Matters for Enterprise AI

Platform and architecture leads are already debating how much of the traditional stack survives the shift to agent-centric systems -- this gives a clean, three-part framing to organize that internal debate, even though it's a thesis rather than a proven roadmap.
