# ContractScrub: A benchmark for final review of legal contracts

arXiv: [2608.20204](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.20204v1) &middot; Published 2026-08-20 &middot; Recommendation: **Priority review** (score 20/25)

_Pre-extraction: this paper has only been screened on its abstract, not read in full yet._

## High-Level Summary

Contract 'scrubbing' -- the final, detail-heavy review of transactional legal agreements for errors and inconsistencies -- is routine, high-volume legal work that looks ideal for LLM automation. ContractScrub is the first formal benchmark testing whether LLMs can actually do it, using expert-crafted test contracts.

## Key Highlights

- First formal evaluation of LLM contract-scrubbing capability, despite the clear economic incentive and apparent fit with frontier-model strengths (long-context reasoning, consistency checking, entity recognition).
- Contracts are hand-crafted by legal experts specifically to test this capability, not scraped from generic legal corpora.
- Directly tests a claim vendors are already making in the market -- that LLMs are ready for high-stakes legal document review.

## Why It Matters for Enterprise AI

Legal ops and compliance leaders evaluating AI vendors for contract review now have a concrete, expert-built test of exactly the claim those vendors are making, rather than having to take marketing claims on faith.

## Business Applications

- Use ContractScrub as a due-diligence test before purchasing or deploying an LLM-based contract review tool.
- Benchmark an internal LLM deployment's contract-review reliability before expanding its scope of use.
- Cite the benchmark results when setting human-in-the-loop requirements for AI-assisted contract review.
