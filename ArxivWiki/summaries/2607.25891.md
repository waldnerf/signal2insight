## Headline

Function calling is solved, coding is improving fastest, and enterprise workflows are still the hardest thing agents do.

## Why triage flagged this

Consolidates 957,253 records over 30 benchmarks and 714 agents into something comparable, and the derived findings are portfolio decisions rather than research results: capability is uneven by task type, with enterprise workflows the least mature -- which is exactly where most deployment budget goes. The second finding is a procurement warning: strict all-pass scoring in multi-verifier tasks can hide progress and reorder agent rankings, so a vendor's position may be an artifact of the aggregation rule. Occupational and industry classification on every record also makes it usable for workforce analysis, not just model selection.

## Triage Detail

**Problem it addresses:** Agent benchmark results are not comparable across scaffolds, verifiers and scoring rules, so there is no reliable basis for deciding where agents are actually ready to deploy.
