## Headline

Fear of falling behind -- not risk appetite -- is what makes teams ship AI unsafely.

## Why triage flagged this

Governance is a priority topic and this reframes AI-safety pressure as a competitive-dynamics problem rather than an individual risk-tolerance one, which is a genuinely new angle for a board-level conversation and highly quotable. Evidence is mixed and the paper says so plainly: the pre-registered comparison across risk levels was not supported, and the interesting results (unsafe choices follow the opponent's, and rise when behind) are exploratory plus a model.
