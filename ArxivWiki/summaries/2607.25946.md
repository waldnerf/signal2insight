## Headline

Stop reviewing every AI label -- review only the ones a cheap model probably got wrong.

## Why triage flagged this

The toxicity finding is incidental; the reusable asset is the labelling architecture -- one call to a small local model producing both a label and interpretable category scores, then a lightweight validator that routes only likely-mislabelled cases to human review. It beats confidence-based and multi-LLM-ensemble baselines at low added cost, which is a direct answer to the LLM-as-judge reliability problem every enterprise evaluation pipeline hits. Demonstrated at real scale (124,000 conversations) and it re-tests prior small-scale claims rather than only asserting new ones. Deployable now with a small local model. || Recalibrated 2026-07-29. Survives as viable because the claim -- stop paying people to re-check every AI label, check only the ones likely wrong -- is a cost and staffing decision that needs no ML background.
