# Inject, Align, Recover: Staged Post-Training for Retrieval-Free Document Knowledge Internalization

arXiv: [2608.20281](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.20281v1) &middot; Published 2026-08-20 &middot; Recommendation: **Review fully** (score 17/25)

_Pre-extraction: this paper has only been screened on its abstract, not read in full yet._

## High-Level Summary

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) has a cost: fetching documents at inference time. This paper proposes IAR, a staged post-training method that instead bakes a bounded document collection directly into a model's parameters, so it can answer questions about that collection without retrieving anything at inference time.

## Key Highlights

- Three-stage recipe (Inject, Align, Recover) separates injecting document knowledge, aligning QA behavior, and recovering general capability lost during specialization.
- Tested across multiple corpora and model families (Llama, Phi, and others), giving evidence this generalizes beyond a single setup.
- Offers a genuine architectural alternative to RAG for bounded, relatively static document sets, not just an incremental RAG tweak.

## Why It Matters for Enterprise AI

For any enterprise running RAG over a large but fairly static internal document set, this raises a real infrastructure-cost question: is it cheaper, over time, to keep paying for retrieval, or to periodically bake the documents into the model instead?

## Business Applications

- Pilot IAR-style internalization for a bounded, slow-changing document set (policies, product docs) as a retrieval-cost reduction test.
- Use the paper's evaluation as a baseline when deciding between RAG and periodic fine-tuning for internal Q&A systems.
- Factor retrieval-free internalization into build-vs-buy conversations about RAG infrastructure spend.
